Questions

Before you send anything.

 

Do I own the song?

Yes. I keep nothing of your record.

How many changes can I ask for?

Two rounds, within 14 days. A third round is $25.

What can I do with a beat pack?

You get a non-exclusive license: release each beat on streaming and social, credit "Prod. Wolf", and keep what you earn. I can sell the same beat to others until someone buys it exclusively. You can't resell the beat itself. If you want it only for you, ask about an exclusive.

Do you master too?

Yes. Add mastering to any mix for $30 per song.

What do I get back?

A full-quality WAV and a normal-loudness MP3. Instrumental and acapella versions are $20 each.

Do you use AI to make the music?

No. The music is yours. I mix and finish it.

Will you post my work?

I may post a short before and after clip unless you tell me no before delivery. A “Mixed by Wolf” credit is requested, not required.

Got a song that’s almost there?

Send it over. I’ll tell you what it needs.
