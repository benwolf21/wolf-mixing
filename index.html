Good to know.

 

Do I own my song?

Yes. For mixing and mastering I keep nothing of your record. For a custom production, release rights, credit and any splits are agreed in writing before I start.

What can I do with a beat license?

A non-exclusive license lets you release the beat on streaming and social, credit “Prod. Wolf”, and keep what you earn. I can license the same beat to others until someone buys it exclusively, and earlier licenses stay valid. You can’t resell the beat itself. Want it only for you? Ask about an exclusive.

Are revisions included?

Yes. Revisions are included.

Do you work in every genre?

Yes. Send me what you’re making and I’ll tell you honestly if it’s a fit.

What do I send for a mix?

A stereo instrumental and your vocal files, or full stems, all starting at the same point, plus a rough mix and a note on what you like and don’t.

Do you use AI to make the music?

No. The music is yours, or it’s made by me.

Will you post my work?

Only with your permission for anything unreleased. A “Mixed by Wolf” credit is requested, not required.
